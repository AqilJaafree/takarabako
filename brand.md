# Brand — Takarabako (宝箱)

_Status: active · chosen in the 2026-09-26 web app brainstorm (concept C: living treasure box + maneki-neko agent)_

## Direction

A lacquered Japanese treasure box. Deep, warm, dark surfaces; lacquer red
for action; gold for money. Money is shown as a physical thing (gold in the
box), not as a dashboard figure. One deliberate theme (dark lacquer); no
light mode.

## Typography

- Display and numerals: **Shippori Mincho B1** (serif, Japanese-rooted).
- Body and UI: **Zen Kaku Gothic New**.
- Addresses, hashes, codes: **JetBrains Mono**.

## Voice

Plain, warm, short. The box and the cat speak like a friendly shopkeeper:
"RM10 is in your box." Not "Deposit transaction submitted."
